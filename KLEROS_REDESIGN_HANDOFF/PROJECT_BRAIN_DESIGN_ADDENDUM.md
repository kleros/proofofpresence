## Persistent design rules

Use the Events visual foundation: Instrument Serif display, Geist body/interface, Geist Mono technical values, original Kleros SVGs, dark background `#17131F`, surface `#201A2B`, body `#E6E1EE`, heading `#F2EEF7`, muted `#A69FB5`, lavender `#A78BFA` with dark text. Exact additional colors, spacing, sizes and provenance are in `design-tokens.json`. Embedded font binaries have complete SIL Open Font License notices; retain them when moving assets. This does not constitute an official brand guide.

Carry stable event identity into every consequential action. Show chain, contract, version and policy when financially relevant. Event switching invalidates stale review plans but must retain pending transaction references. Do not derive authorization from displayed roles alone.

Model admission, deposit receipt, contract registration, personal history, staff observation and financial eligibility independently. A host-approved spot or successful payment alone does not prove the other states. Provide one eligible next action with a reason when blocked. Preserve free participation and private content permissions.

Keep legacy v7 native ETH/Kleros V1 semantics through versioned adapters. Ordinary payments may proceed while matched cases hold shares. Display transferred, reserved and owed balances together. Jury votes, final resolver outcomes, settlement and confirmed transfers are separate. Preserve appeals, owed claims, cancellation limits and no-list recovery. This design authorizes no financial protocol migration.

Imports require provenance, event/deployment binding, actual identity/deposit reconciliation, exception review, exclusions, consequence preview and revalidation before signing. A manual acknowledgment cannot override a proven event mismatch. Newsletter consent, confirmed subscription, merchandise and attendance authority stay separate. Demo queues must not later flush synthetic personal records to live services.

Use 44px proposed primary targets, visible labels/focus, keyboard tabs/dialogs, announced asynchronous results, and layouts that stack at 900px and 600px. Validate actual responsive behavior in the integrated application.
